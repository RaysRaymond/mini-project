## Project Overview

> Briefly describe your project idea, what problem it solves, and what you aim to build.
-My project idea is that I'm trying to build a health awareness website that tracks your major health factors that may influence you and try to give advices that are creditable long term and short term. The problem it solves is that most people who may felt like they have issues with their health could try and find online websites that actually guides them to live a better life through advices provided by my website. Additionally, I also plan to build a forum in the website so that everyone can interact with each other and share their life experiences in the topic of quality of life based on what they do and what are the results and what do they think as a whole community.
